About SAIMS

Better records support better schools.

The School Asset and Inventory Management System replaces scattered spreadsheets and paper trails with a consistent digital workflow. It helps school administrators know what the school owns, where each item is located, who is accountable, and what needs attention.

Designed around day-to-day school operations, SAIMS makes accurate inventory management approachable without sacrificing detail.
